# Red Bull Events — from discovery to tickets

**Service.** Red Bull Events, the brand’s public event calendar and event pages.

**Workflow.** Help a U.S. spectator find an upcoming event they can attend and reach the official ticket or RSVP page. The proposed path is to choose a location and date range, browse by interest, check the venue and admission details, and open the spectator ticket or RSVP link.

## Three references

| Reference | What I observed | Implication for the workflow |
|---|---|---|
| **Red Bull Events** · existing service | The U.S. calendar supports country, discipline, and event-status filters. Listings show dates, venues, and statuses such as tickets available or registration open. | Keep browsing by interest and make the spectator’s next step explicit. [Red Bull Events](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/events) |
| **Monster Energy Events** · direct brand peer | Its calendar groups events under Motor and Lifestyle and shows locations and dates alongside a general “Learn More” link. | Use dates and venues to make listings easy to scan. Use a specific action such as “Get spectator tickets” when available. [Monster Energy Events](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-us/events/) |
| **Eventbrite** · adjacent event marketplace | Its Newark page groups listings by timeframe and shows the time, venue, and a link to check ticket price. | Give visitors a city and date entry point so they can quickly judge whether an event fits their plans. [Eventbrite Newark](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/nj--newark/events/) |

## Two positions

| Position | Promise | What it prioritizes |
|---|---|---|
| **Attend a Red Bull event** | “Find a Red Bull event you can go to and see how to get in.” | Location, date, admission details, and a clear spectator ticket or RSVP link. |
| **Compete in a Red Bull event** | “Find a Red Bull competition you’re eligible to enter and see how to qualify.” | Eligibility, deadlines, qualifier formats, and registration. |

## Two persona hypotheses

- **Spectator making plans with friends.** May browse across sports, gaming, and music, then decide based on travel distance, timing, and admission. Test whether they can find an event that fits their plans and identify the correct ticket action.
- **Aspiring competitor.** May arrive with a specific game or discipline in mind and first check whether they qualify. Test whether they can identify an eligible qualifier and explain how to enter.

**Audience choice.** Start with **U.S. spectators looking for an in-person event**. Red Bull already lists ticketed events across different interests, so the same discovery-to-ticket workflow can serve several kinds of events. Wings Cup illustrates where a clear spectator path matters. Its FAQ gives spectators an RSVP link and gives competitors separate qualifier instructions. That distinction supports the design choice. The persona motivations are hypotheses based on the public pages and still need testing with users.
