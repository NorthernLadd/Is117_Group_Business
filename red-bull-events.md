# Red Bull Events

## 1. Research

**Project** — Red Bull Events, the brand’s public event calendar and event pages.

**Research date** — November 15, 2026.

**Workflow** — Find a Red Bull event to attend in person. A spectator chooses a city and date range, browses by interest, checks the venue and admission details, and opens the official spectator ticket or RSVP link.

### My competitive landscape

I compared the current Red Bull experience, a competing energy-drink brand’s event calendar, and an adjacent marketplace for local event discovery.

| Source | Site | Observation | Decision it suggests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | [Red Bull Events](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/events) · current service | The calendar filters by country, discipline, and event status. Event cards show dates, venues, and labels such as tickets available or registrations open. | Keep browsing by discipline and make the spectator action explicit when an event also has a competitor registration path. |
| R2 | [Monster Energy Events](https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-us/events/) · direct brand peer | The calendar groups listings under Motor and Lifestyle. Listings show dates and locations and use a general Learn More link. | Make date and venue easy to scan. Use a specific link such as Get Spectator Tickets when the destination is known. |
| R3 | [Eventbrite Newark](https://www.eventbrite.com/d/nj--newark/events/) · adjacent marketplace | The Newark page groups listings by timeframe and displays event times, venues, and ticket-price links. | Let spectators narrow by city and date before opening individual listings. |

**My proposed niche** — U.S. spectators looking for an in-person Red Bull event, including people making plans with friends.

**Promise** — Find a Red Bull event that fits your plans and see how to get in.

**Source → decision** — Eventbrite groups local listings by timeframe (R3), and Red Bull already shows event dates, venues, and disciplines (R1). Start with city and date filters, then show the venue, admission details, and spectator ticket or RSVP action. The Wings Cup FAQ provides a concrete example of distinct spectator and competitor actions, with an RSVP for spectators and bracket registration for players. [Wings Cup rules and FAQ](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/events/red-bull-wings-cup-united-states-2026/red-bull-wings-cup-usa-rules-faq)

**Still uncertain** — How often spectators start with a city and date, how far they will travel, and which admission details they check first.

### Two positions I considered

- **Position A · Attend a Red Bull event.** Help spectators find an event and get in. Prioritize location, date, admission details, and spectator tickets or RSVP.
- **Position B · Compete in a Red Bull event.** Help entrants find a competition they’re eligible for and understand how to qualify. Prioritize eligibility, deadlines, qualifiers, and registration.

### Two persona cards

| | Persona A · Spectator making plans with friends | Persona B · Aspiring competitor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Situation | A U.S. spectator is planning an in-person outing with friends and is open to several interests. | A player or athlete is looking for an event in a specific discipline. |
| Goal | Find a Red Bull event the group can attend and reach the official ticket or RSVP page. | Find an eligible qualifier and figure out how to enter. |
| Concern | Can we get there, does the date work, and what do we need to get in? | Do I qualify, and when does registration close? |
| Information needed | Date, venue, ticket or RSVP status, and any entry requirements. | Eligibility, registration deadline, qualifier format, and entry instructions. |
| Evidence / assumption | Red Bull publishes ticketed listings across disciplines. Planning with friends and browsing across interests are hypotheses. | The Wings Cup FAQ describes qualifier registration. The entrant’s priorities are hypotheses. |

### My audience choice

I chose **U.S. spectators looking for an in-person Red Bull event**. Red Bull lists ticketed events across several disciplines, and the same workflow applies each time. A spectator can check when and where an event takes place, confirm admission details, and reach the ticket or RSVP page. Competitive entry can require event-specific eligibility and qualifier rules, as Wings Cup illustrates. [Red Bull Events](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/events) · [Wings Cup rules and FAQ](https://www.redbull.com/us-en/events/red-bull-wings-cup-united-states-2026/red-bull-wings-cup-usa-rules-faq)

**Research limits** — The evidence comes from public pages. Demand and audience preferences remain open questions. To test the spectator hypothesis, ask people planning an outing to find an event that fits their schedule and show where they would get tickets.
